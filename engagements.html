Transparent fees

Your management fee is set in a written proposal.

We use custom proposals for now. After discovery, you’ll see the monthly fee and exactly what it covers before deciding whether to proceed.

Always separate from management

  • Advertising spend is paid directly by you to Meta or TikTok through your ad account.
  • Software subscriptions, creator or talent fees, production and other third-party costs are itemized separately when needed.
  • No spend or outside cost is authorized without your approval.

Terms agreed before work begins

  • The proposal states scope, monthly fee, billing cadence, responsibilities and reporting schedule.
  • Any initial term, renewal approach and cancellation notice are written into the proposal before acceptance.
  • Larger or multi-market programs can receive a tailored proposal with phased scope and named workstreams.
