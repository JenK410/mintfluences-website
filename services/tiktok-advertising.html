What we deliver

Practical work, scoped around your priorities.

We define scope, responsibilities and volume in a written proposal before work begins. The deliverables below describe the service areas available for your engagement.

Service deliverables

  • Channel-fit assessment based on audience, offer, creative capacity and conversion path.
  • Campaign strategy, objective selection and account structure for agreed goals.
  • Audience and targeting recommendations, including retargeting where eligible and appropriate.
  • Platform-native creative concepts, scripts or briefs that support a structured test plan.
  • Campaign setup, monitoring and optimization against agreed measures.
  • Performance review covering spend, creative learning, conversion signals and next steps.

How onboarding works

  1. Review audience fit, commercial goals, offer, landing experience and available creative resources.
  2. Confirm TikTok Ads Manager access, event setup and any catalog or pixel requirements.
  3. Agree on creative approvals, production responsibilities, testing volume and launch budget.
  4. Set the first testing roadmap and reporting cadence in the approved proposal.

Clear scope

Included in the agreed service. Priced separately when needed.

Your proposal will identify deliverables, client responsibilities and any third-party or project costs before approval.

Included in scope

  • Strategy, planning and management for the selected channel, within the volume set out in your proposal.
  • Routine optimization, quality checks and a performance review on the agreed reporting cadence.

  • Product samples, shipping, promotional incentives and other campaign costs.
Frequently asked questions

Good to know before we begin.

Do you make TikTok videos?

Creative concepts and production briefs can be included. Filming, creator sourcing, editing and usage rights are listed separately unless the proposal specifically includes them.

Is TikTok suitable for every business?

Not always. We assess audience fit, offer, conversion path and available creative capacity before recommending an engagement.

Are TikTok ad costs included?

No. The client pays TikTok media spend directly through its ad account, separate from agency management fees.

How do you measure TikTok performance?

We review platform reporting alongside available site or commerce analytics, explain attribution limitations and use agreed business indicators. No sales or return is guaranteed.
