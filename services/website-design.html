What we deliver

A website shaped around how your business works.

We scope the pages, platform, functionality, content needs and review steps with you before design and development begin. The result is a clear digital foundation that can support customer discovery and action.

Design and implementation

  • Custom business website design aligned with your brand and audience.
  • Website development and implementation for the agreed platform and scope.
  • WordPress website design, new builds and redesigns.
  • Landing page design for a focused offer, campaign or lead-generation goal.

  • Website updates and ongoing maintenance options, defined separately in your proposal.

How onboarding works

  1. Discuss your business goals, current site, customer journey and project constraints.
  2. Confirm platform, page list, functionality, content responsibilities and project milestones.
  3. Review the proposed structure and design direction before development proceeds.
  4. Build, review and refine the agreed pages, then complete responsive and launch checks.
  5. Publish or hand off the completed work, with any ongoing support defined in writing.

Clear scope

Know what is included before work begins.

Your proposal will identify deliverables, review rounds, responsibilities and third-party costs. Changes outside the agreed scope are discussed before additional work proceeds.

Included in the agreed scope

  • Discovery, planning and design for the agreed pages and functions.
  • Implementation, responsive checks and the agreed revision and approval stages.
  • Basic page title and description setup for pages included in the project.
  • Launch support or handoff as described in the proposal.

Priced separately when needed

  • Domain registration, hosting, premium themes, plugins and other third-party subscriptions.
  • Copywriting, photography, video, illustration, translation or other content production unless listed in scope.
  • Complex custom integrations, e-commerce setup, migrations or technical remediation beyond the agreed work.
  • Ongoing updates, maintenance and new pages beyond any agreed monthly support allowance.
Frequently asked questions

Good to know before we begin.

Can you design or redesign a WordPress website?

Yes. WordPress design, new builds and redesigns are available. We confirm the current setup, required functionality and platform responsibilities during discovery.

Will the website work on mobile?

Responsive behavior is part of the agreed design and implementation scope. We review agreed pages across common screen sizes before launch or handoff.

Are hosting and domain fees included?

Domain, hosting, premium plugin and other third-party fees are separate unless your written proposal explicitly includes them.

Can you maintain the website after launch?

Yes. Website updates and ongoing maintenance can be scoped as a separate recurring service with defined tasks, response expectations and monthly limits.

How long will a website project take?

Timing depends on page count, functionality, content readiness, review turnaround and any existing-site migration needs. We set milestone expectations in the proposal after discovery.

Can you connect the website to marketing campaigns?

Yes. Website design can be combined with email marketing or Meta and TikTok advertising. The inquiry form lets you describe a website-only or combined-service request.
