What we deliver

Practical work, scoped around your priorities.

We define scope, responsibilities and volume in a written proposal before work begins. The deliverables below describe the service areas available for your engagement.

Service deliverables

  • Account and campaign review with prioritized opportunities and tracking considerations.
  • Campaign strategy, structure and setup for agreed objectives and markets.
  • Audience research and targeting recommendations, including prospecting and retargeting where appropriate and permitted.
  • Creative concepts and testing briefs built around customer motivations, offers and placements.
  • Campaign monitoring and optimization against agreed indicators and approved budget.
  • Reporting that separates platform-reported outcomes from broader business results and attribution limits.

How onboarding works

  1. Review business economics, goals, products, markets and the current paid-media setup.
  2. Confirm access to Meta Business Suite, ad account, pixel or event sources and available analytics.

Clear scope

Included in the agreed service. Priced separately when needed.

Your proposal will identify deliverables, client responsibilities and any third-party or project costs before approval.

Included in scope

  • Strategy, planning and management for the selected channel, within the volume set out in your proposal.
  • Routine optimization, quality checks and a performance review on the agreed reporting cadence.

Frequently asked questions

Good to know before we begin.

Is ad spend part of the management fee?

No. Media spend is paid to Meta through the client’s ad account and is separate from agency management fees.

Do you create the ads?

We can provide concepts, messaging and testing briefs. The proposal will state whether asset production, editing or creator coordination is included or quoted separately.

Can you guarantee a cost per lead or return on ad spend?

No. Results depend on market conditions, offer, site experience, budget, tracking and other factors. We set measurable objectives and make evidence-based adjustments, without guaranteeing a specific outcome.

What budget should we start with?

We recommend a budget after reviewing your goals, economics, audience size and testing needs. Spend is approved by you and paid directly to the platform.
