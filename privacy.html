Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

This notice describes the current Mintfluences website and inquiry process. Privacy obligations can depend on where visitors and the business are located and on how the site is operated.

Information you choose to provide

The inquiry form asks for your name, business name, work email, website or social profile, industry, approximate monthly paid media budget, advertising status, marketing goals and any additional context you choose to share. Please do not include sensitive personal information.

How the inquiry form works

This static website does not send form data to a website database or CRM. When you submit the form, it prepares a message in your device’s email application addressed to hello@mintfluences.com. Nothing is sent until you review and send that email. Your email provider and the receiving mail service then handle the message under their own practices. If you do not want to use email, you can contact the address directly.

Website hosting and third-party assets

The site is hosted using GitHub Pages. It also loads fonts from Google Fonts and photographs from Unsplash. Those providers may receive standard technical information such as an IP address and browser request details when your browser requests their services. Please review their policies for details about their handling of those requests.

Cookies and analytics

This version of the website does not intentionally include advertising or analytics tracking. Hosting and third-party services may use technical logs or mechanisms needed to deliver and protect their services.

Use and retention

If you send an inquiry, Mintfluences uses the message to respond and discuss a potential engagement. Email is retained in the relevant mail systems according to their settings and applicable business needs. The site itself does not retain a copy of the draft.

Your questions or requests

For a privacy question or to request access, correction or deletion of an inquiry you sent, email hello@mintfluences.com. Requests will be handled subject to applicable law and any records that must be retained.

This page describes the current site’s actual inquiry flow; it is not a determination that one policy satisfies every legal requirement. Privacy requirements vary by location and business activity.
