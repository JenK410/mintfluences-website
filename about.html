About Mintfluences

Strategy that respects the business behind the brand.

Mintfluences helps growing businesses approach email and paid social with clearer priorities, thoughtful execution and honest measurement. We connect the work to your goals, customer journey and operating realities.

Our mission

Make digital marketing more useful to the people running the business.

Good marketing is a series of informed decisions. Our role is to help identify the next useful decision, carry it through carefully and explain what we learn.

Clarity before complexity. A clear objective, a considered plan and an honest read of the evidence are the foundation of every engagement.

Our values

Principles that guide the work.

01 / CLARITY

Make the work understandable

We define the objective, scope, owners and review points before launch, and make reporting useful for decisions.

02 / CARE

Respect customers and context

We plan for the people receiving the message or seeing the ad, with attention to relevance, consent and the customer experience.

03 / ACCOUNTABILITY

Say what the evidence shows

We distinguish observed platform data from broader business outcomes, identify limitations and avoid guaranteed results.

Our approach

Understand, plan, execute, learn.

The process stays practical and collaborative, with decisions made in context and responsibilities agreed before work begins.

Start with the business

  • Understand goals, customer economics, current channel activity and constraints.
  • Choose a focused plan with agreed success indicators.

Improve with evidence

  • Build and launch with clear tracking, creative direction and approval steps.
  • Review performance, explain what changed and prioritize the next action.
